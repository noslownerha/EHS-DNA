Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026 · Last updated October 1, 2026

In plain terms: we collect what’s needed to run a safety program — who you are at work, and the safety records your company keeps. Your employer controls that information; we look after it on their behalf. We don’t sell it, we don’t advertise with it, and we don’t track you around the web.

1. Who this covers

This policy explains how Joseph McRory LLC, doing business as EHS DNA (“we”, “us”), handles personal information in the EHS DNA service and its websites. EHS DNA is used by businesses (our “Customers”) to manage workplace safety for their people (“Users”).

Your employer is in charge of your information in EHS DNA. The Customer decides what is recorded and who can see it; we process that information for them, under our Terms of Service. If you have a question about information your employer keeps about you, please contact them first — we will help them respond.

2. What we collect

Account information

Name, work email address, role, site and department — set up by your employer’s administrator. Your password is stored only as a one-way cryptographic hash; we can’t see it.

Safety records your company keeps

  • Incident reports, which can include who was involved, what happened, and details of injuries or illnesses. This can be sensitive health information, so access is restricted by role — for example, staff cannot see other people’s injury reports.
  • Hazards, findings, inspections, corrective actions and their history.
  • Photos people choose to attach.
  • Location only when someone taps “Tag my current location” on a report — never in the background — and pins placed on a site map.
  • Training assignments, quiz results, completions and certificates; equipment, maintenance and procedures; recognition points.

Technical information

When the app is used, our servers record basic information needed to run and secure it — such as IP address, time, and the request made — in server logs.

On your device

The app keeps your signed-in session, and any reports saved while you were offline (until they send), in your browser’s storage. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies, and there are no third-party trackers in the app.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the service your employer subscribes to: recording and routing safety information, sending notifications and reminders, producing reports.
  • To keep the service secure — for example, locking an account after repeated failed sign-ins — and to investigate problems.
  • To support your employer when they ask for help. Support access is visibly shown in the app while it’s active.
  • To test updates against real data before release, in a separate, password-protected copy of the service with outgoing email switched off. That copy is overwritten each time we test.
  • To improve the service using de-identified, aggregated information that doesn’t identify any person or company.

We never sell personal information, use it for advertising, or share it for others’ marketing.

5. How long we keep it

  • For as long as your employer’s subscription is active, according to their settings. Records such as injury reports are kept because employers often have legal duties to retain them.
  • After a subscription ends, your employer can request an export for 30 days; we then delete the data from the live service. Backup copies expire and are deleted within 12 months.
  • Server logs are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting.

6. How we protect it

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS, with browsers instructed to always use it).
  • Passwords are hashed, and accounts lock temporarily after repeated failed sign-ins.
  • Each company’s data is kept strictly separate, and that separation is tested automatically before every update.
  • Access inside a company follows the roles its administrator assigns; changes to key safety records are logged with who made them and when.
  • Our own access is limited to support and security, and is visibly flagged in the app.
  • Data is backed up nightly.

No system is perfectly secure. If we confirm a breach affecting your company’s information, we will notify your company without undue delay.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, or to object to certain uses. Because your employer controls your information in EHS DNA, please make these requests to them; we will help them respond. You can also write to us at info@ehsdna.com and we will pass your request to your employer or answer it directly where we are responsible. We won’t treat you differently for exercising your rights.

8. International transfers

EHS DNA is hosted in the European Union and used mainly in the United States. Some of our service providers are in the United States. Where the law requires it, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

9. Children

EHS DNA is a workplace tool and is not intended for children under 16.

We will post any changes here with a new date, and tell Customer administrators about material changes in advance.
