Common questions

The things you're probably wondering.

How long does implementation take?

Days, not months. We pre-load your sites, departments, staff list, inspection checklists, and training matrix before your first login. Most companies are taking live reports within the first week.

Will our floor staff actually use it?

That's the entire design premise. Reporting is a guided wizard on any phone browser — no app install, under two minutes end to end. If a new hire can't use it on day one, we consider that our bug.

What does it cost?

Simple annual pricing scaled to your operation — a fraction of legacy enterprise platforms, with every module included. Tell us your site and headcount and we'll quote it on the demo call, no procurement maze.

Is our data safe?

Your data lives in an isolated tenant with encrypted connections (HTTPS), role-based access, and automatic nightly backups with 30-day retention. You own your data and can export it anytime.

Can it handle multiple sites with different needs?

It's built for exactly that. Checklists, floor plans, training schedules, and dashboards are all per-site — with a company-wide rollup for leadership.

We already have spreadsheets and a process. Why switch?

Spreadsheets don't notify anyone, don't escalate overdue items, don't work on a phone at the incident scene, and don't survive an audit gracefully. Keep your process — EHS DNA just makes it run itself.
